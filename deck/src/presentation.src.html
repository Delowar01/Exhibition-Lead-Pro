How to take part

We recommend raw space, not a shell-scheme booth

Recommended

Raw space

Beez rents an empty floor area. Walls, branding, lighting, flooring, furniture and AV are designed and built for Beez.
It carries weight and height — so a chilled display unit, a reefer-panel mock-up or a live tracking wall can be built in properly, with the power and floor loading they need.
Available from 9 sqm up to 160 sqm, so the size follows the objective rather than the system.

Shell scheme

A ready-made booth: frame walls, a table, chairs, carpet and spotlights.
Branding is limited to panel graphics. No structural demo, no cold-chain equipment, no height.
Fine for a small trading presence — not for introducing Beez to the Saudi market for the first time.
Capped at 24 sqm, which is below the size we recommend, and it costs more per square metre than raw space, not less.

Brand control

The structure is designed around Beez rather than adapted to a system booth.

Visibility

Height, lighting and open sides make the stand readable from across the aisle.

Visitor journey

Reception, cold-chain demo and meeting areas are placed in a deliberate sequence.

Premium finish

Custom materials and graphics instead of a standard shell-scheme frame.

For comparison: the organizer’s shell scheme is offered from 9–24 sqm only, priced at raw space + $90 / sqm (SAR 338 / sqm), with a table, chairs, carpet and spotlights included.
Raw space gives design control and stronger visitor impact
Beez has never exhibited, so define both terms in plain words before comparing them: shell scheme is a booth you rent ready-made, raw space is an empty piece of floor you build on. The decisive point is physical, not aesthetic — a shell scheme cannot take the weight, power or height of a chilled display or a reefer panel, so the one thing that makes Beez memorable on that floor cannot be built inside it. Note that shell scheme is capped at 24 sqm anyway, below the size we recommend.
