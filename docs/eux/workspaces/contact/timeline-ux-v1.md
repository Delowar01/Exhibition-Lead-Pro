# Contact Timeline UX v1.0

Status: Approved
Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Timeline is the complete history of a customer relationship.

It answers one question:

> "What has happened throughout this relationship?"

Unlike the Overview, which summarizes information,
Timeline becomes the single source of truth.

Every interaction should be visible here.

---

# 2. UX Principles

Timeline must be:

✓ Easy to scan

✓ Chronological

✓ Filterable

✓ Searchable

✓ Actionable

✓ Never overwhelming

Users should understand months of customer history within seconds.

# 22. UX Rules

Timeline is always chronological.

Timeline never hides history.

Timeline never duplicates records.

Timeline groups related events.

Timeline never requires more than two clicks
to reach event details.

Timeline is the source of truth.

Overview summarizes.

Timeline explains.
