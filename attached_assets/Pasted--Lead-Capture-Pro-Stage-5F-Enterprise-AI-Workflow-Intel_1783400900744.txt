# Lead Capture Pro
# Stage 5F – Enterprise AI Workflow Intelligence

Objective

Stage 5E – Enterprise Intelligent Capture has been successfully completed and approved.

Proceed with Stage 5F – Enterprise AI Workflow Intelligence.

The objective is to transform Lead Capture Pro from a passive CRM into an intelligent CRM that proactively monitors activities, identifies opportunities and risks, recommends next actions, and automates repetitive business workflows while keeping users fully in control.

This phase builds upon:

✅ Stage 5.0 – AI Platform Foundation
✅ Stage 5A – Enterprise AI Lead & Customer Intelligence
✅ Stage 5B – Enterprise AI Sales Copilot
✅ Stage 5C – Executive Intelligence
✅ Stage 5E – Enterprise Intelligent Capture

Do NOT begin Stage 5D (Enterprise AI Assistant).

=========================================================

Requirements

Preserve:

• Enterprise Privacy
• Multi-tenancy
• RBAC
• Contract-first Architecture
• Repository Layer
• Service Layer
• Existing CRM
• Existing AI Platform
• Existing OCR
• Existing Sales Copilot
• Existing Executive Intelligence

No regressions.

No breaking APIs.

No breaking database changes unless required for workflow intelligence.

=========================================================

Enterprise AI Principles

AI recommends.

Users decide.

Nothing should automatically:

• Change CRM records
• Send messages
• Assign leads
• Close opportunities
• Delete records

Every recommendation must include:

• Confidence Score
• Reasoning
• Model
• Prompt Version
• Generated Date
• Suggested Action

Every accepted workflow action must be audited.
