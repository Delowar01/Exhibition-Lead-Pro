# DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD – DELETE FUNCTIONALITY FIX

## Project

AI Exhibition Lead Capture Platform

## Scope

**MOBILE APPLICATION ONLY**

(No Admin Panel changes)

Before implementing, review the existing Digital Business Card workflow to ensure no regression occurs.

---

# Current Issue

The **Delete** button in the **Workspace → My Digital Business Card** screen is currently non-functional.

Clicking the Delete button does nothing.

This functionality must be completed.

---

# Required Implementation

## Delete Digital Business Card

The Delete button must permanently delete the user's Digital Business Card from the system.

Deleting the card should remove:

* Digital Business Card record
* Public sharing token
* Public Digital Business Card page
* QR Code data
* Published status
* Public/Hidden field settings

The user's account and profile must **NOT** be deleted.

Only the Digital Business Card should be removed.

---

# Confirmation Dialog

Before deleting, display a native confirmation dialog.

### Title

**Delete Digital Business Card**

### Message

**Are you sure you want to delete your Digital Business Card?**

**This action cannot be undone.**

### Buttons

**Cancel**

* Close the dialog.
* No data is deleted.

**Delete**

* Permanently delete the Digital Business Card.
* Close the dialog after successful deletion.

Use the platform-native confirmation dialog:

* Android Material Alert Dialog
* iOS Native Alert

---

# After Successful Deletion

After the Digital Business Card is deleted:

1. Display a success message.

Example:

> **Your Digital Business Card has been deleted successfully.**

2. Automatically navigate back to the Workspace.

3. The Workspace should now display the empty state:

**Create Your Digital Business Card**

instead of the existing card.

4. The Home Dashboard **My Digital Business Card** button should remain visible.

If the user taps it after deletion, display:

> **You haven't created your Digital Business Card yet.**

Provide a single action button:

**Go to Workspace**

which opens:

**Workspace → My Digital Business Card**

---

# Error Handling

If deletion fails:

* Display an error message.
* Do not remove the card from the UI.
* Allow the user to retry.

Example:

> **Unable to delete your Digital Business Card. Please try again.**

---

# Security

Users must only be able to delete **their own** Digital Business Card.

Deleting another user's card must never be possible.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The task will be considered complete only when:

* The Delete button is fully functional.
* A native confirmation dialog appears before deletion.
* The dialog displays:

  * **Delete Digital Business Card**
  * **Are you sure you want to delete your Digital Business Card?**
  * **This action cannot be undone.**
* **Cancel** closes the dialog without deleting.
* **Delete** permanently removes the Digital Business Card.
* The public sharing page and QR Code become invalid immediately after deletion.
* The user is returned to the Workspace with the "Create Your Digital Business Card" empty state.
* The Home Dashboard behaves correctly after deletion.
* Proper success and error messages are displayed.
* The feature is tested on both Android and iPhone.
* Screenshots and testing evidence are provided before marking the task as complete.
