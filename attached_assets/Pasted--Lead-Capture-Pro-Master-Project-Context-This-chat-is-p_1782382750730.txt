# Development Rule

Until Stage 2.11 is fully completed and approved:

* Do NOT introduce architectural changes that conflict with Stage 2.11.
* Do NOT bypass tenant isolation.
* Do NOT redesign authentication.
* Do NOT redesign authorization.
* Do NOT redesign repositories.
* Do NOT redesign services.
* Preserve all APIs.
* Preserve all database contracts.
* Preserve all mobile compatibility.

If any requested feature would conflict with Stage 2.11, complete Stage 2.11 first.
