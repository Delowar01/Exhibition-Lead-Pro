# HOME DASHBOARD IMPROVEMENT – REPLACE DIGITAL CARD WITH ACTION BUTTON

## Project

AI Exhibition Lead Capture Platform

## Scope

**MOBILE APPLICATION ONLY**

(No Admin Panel changes)

---

# Current Issue

The Home Dashboard is still displaying the full **Digital Business Card**.

This is **not** the intended design.

The Home Dashboard should remain a dashboard for lead management and analytics, not a place to permanently display the Digital Business Card.

The Digital Business Card should only be displayed when the user explicitly chooses to view it.

---

# Required Change

## Remove

Remove the entire Digital Business Card from the Home Dashboard.

This includes:

* Profile photo
* Name
* Designation
* Company
* QR Code
* "Tap to share or edit"
* Any Digital Card preview

Nothing related to the Digital Business Card should be permanently visible on the Home screen.

---

# Replace With

Replace the current card with a compact premium action button, similar to the previous **Capture a Lead** card.

The new card should have the following:

### Title

**My Digital Business Card**

### Subtitle

**View and share your digital business card**

### Icon

Use a premium Digital Business Card or QR Card icon.

Do **not** use the scan icon.

### Right Arrow

Keep the arrow on the right side to indicate navigation.

### Design

Use the same:

* Width
* Height
* Border radius
* Shadow
* Orange gradient
* Premium styling

as the previous **Capture a Lead** action card.

The goal is to keep the Home Dashboard clean while still providing quick access to the Digital Business Card.

---

# Navigation

When the user taps **My Digital Business Card**, navigate to the dedicated Digital Business Card screen.

Do **not** open a popup.

Do **not** display the card inline.

Navigate to a separate page.

---

# Digital Business Card Screen

The Digital Business Card page should remain exactly as designed previously.

Display the card in a **vertical portrait layout**.

This page should include:

* Profile photo
* Name
* Designation
* Company
* Contact details
* QR Code
* Share functionality

The Home screen should never display this full layout.

---

# Editing

The Digital Business Card screen should remain **View & Share only**.

Do **not** show an Edit button.

Editing must only be available from:

**Workspace → My Digital Business Card**

This ensures a single location for managing card information.

---

# Home Dashboard Layout

The Home Dashboard order should be:

1. Greeting
2. User Name
3. User Role
4. Company Name
5. **My Digital Business Card** action button
6. Dashboard widgets
7. Last Event report
8. Remaining dashboard content

No Digital Business Card preview should appear on the dashboard.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The implementation will be considered complete only when:

* The full Digital Business Card is completely removed from the Home Dashboard.
* It is replaced with a compact **My Digital Business Card** action button.
* The button maintains the premium design previously used for **Capture a Lead**.
* Tapping the button opens the dedicated Digital Business Card page.
* The Digital Business Card page remains in **vertical portrait orientation**.
* The Digital Business Card page supports **View** and **Share** only.
* Editing is available **only** through **Workspace → My Digital Business Card**.
* The Home Dashboard remains clean, uncluttered, and optimized for lead management.
* Screenshots of the updated Home Dashboard and Digital Business Card page are provided before marking the task complete.
