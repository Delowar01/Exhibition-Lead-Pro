# Lead Capture Pro – Enterprise Privacy & Data Governance Audit

## Objective

Conduct a comprehensive **Enterprise Privacy & Data Governance Audit** of the entire Lead Capture Pro platform.

This is an **audit and verification task first**, not a feature development task.

The purpose is to verify that the platform follows enterprise SaaS best practices where the **Platform Owner (Elite Marcom)** can fully operate the SaaS platform **without having routine access to customer business data**.

If any gaps are found, identify them, classify their severity, and recommend remediation. Only implement fixes after documenting and verifying them.

---

# Background

Lead Capture Pro is an enterprise multi-tenant SaaS platform.

There are two completely separate administrative levels:

## Level 1 — Platform Owner (Elite Marcom)

Responsible for:

* Platform management
* Company onboarding
* Subscription management
* Billing
* Licensing
* Infrastructure
* Platform monitoring
* Queue monitoring
* Security monitoring
* System maintenance
* Global configuration

Platform Owner is **NOT** a customer.

Platform Owner should manage the platform itself, **not customer business data**.

---

## Level 2 — Customer Company

Each customer owns its own isolated environment.

Examples of customer-owned data include:

* Contacts
* Leads
* Business Cards
* OCR Results
* Meeting Notes
* Events
* Follow-ups
* Sales Pipeline
* Reports
* Analytics
* AI Results
* Attachments
* Imported Files
* Exported Data
* User Activity
* Company Documents

Only authorized users belonging to that company may access this information.

---

# Enterprise Privacy Policy

The platform must follow the Standard Enterprise SaaS Privacy Model.

---

## Platform Owner CAN access

Platform operational information only.

Examples:

* Company profile
* Subscription
* License
* Billing
* User counts
* Storage usage
* API usage
* Queue health
* Background jobs
* Security alerts
* Audit summaries
* System metrics
* Infrastructure health
* Feature flags
* Platform configuration

---

## Platform Owner MUST NOT access

Unless a future support-access feature is intentionally implemented.

Platform Owner must not be able to:

* Browse customer contacts
* Search customer leads
* View customer pipelines
* Open customer reports
* Read customer notes
* View customer meetings
* Read OCR results
* View business cards
* Download attachments
* Export customer data
* View customer analytics
* Read AI-generated customer insights
* Access customer dashboards
* Read customer communications

---

# Audit Scope

Perform a complete review of the platform.

---

## 1. Authentication Review

Verify:

* Platform authentication
* Customer authentication
* Session separation
* Refresh tokens
* JWT scopes
* MFA enforcement
* Company isolation

---

## 2. Authorization Review

Review every permission.

Verify Platform Owner cannot inherit customer permissions.

Verify customer users cannot inherit platform permissions.

Review all role mappings.

---

## 3. Repository Layer Review

Inspect every repository.

Verify repositories enforce tenant isolation.

Ensure Platform Owner cannot query customer business data through repository classes.

---

## 4. Service Layer Review

Inspect every service.

Verify all business services require proper tenant context.

Identify any service capable of bypassing tenant isolation.

---

## 5. API Review

Inspect every API endpoint.

Verify:

* Authentication
* Authorization
* Tenant validation
* Company validation
* Ownership validation

Identify endpoints exposing customer information to Platform Owner.

---

## 6. Platform Dashboard Review

Review every page available to Platform Owner.

Confirm the dashboard only exposes platform operational information.

Verify no page can browse customer CRM data.

---

## 7. Customer Dashboard Review

Verify every customer only sees their own tenant.

Confirm:

* Contacts
* Leads
* Events
* Reports
* Analytics
* Attachments

cannot cross tenant boundaries.

---

## 8. Search Review

Verify Platform Owner search only searches platform metadata.

It must never search:

* contacts
* leads
* OCR
* notes
* business cards
* meetings

belonging to customers.

---

## 9. Reports Review

Verify Platform Owner cannot:

* open reports
* view analytics
* inspect customer dashboards

unless a future support-access mechanism is explicitly implemented.

---

## 10. Export Review

Review every export endpoint.

Verify Platform Owner cannot export customer CRM data.

---

## 11. Background Jobs Review

Verify queue workers cannot leak tenant data.

Verify background jobs always execute within tenant boundaries.

---

## 12. Notifications Review

Verify notifications remain tenant scoped.

Platform notifications must remain separate from customer notifications.

---

## 13. Logging Review

Review all application logs.

Verify sensitive customer information is not unnecessarily written to logs.

Ensure secrets, passwords, tokens, and confidential customer information remain protected.

---

## 14. Audit Log Review

Verify every privileged Platform Owner action is recorded.

Examples:

* Company created
* Company suspended
* Subscription modified
* Billing updated
* Security configuration changed
* User disabled
* Platform configuration updated

---

# Security Review

Perform a complete security review.

Check for:

* Privilege escalation
* Broken access control
* IDOR vulnerabilities
* Cross-tenant access
* Missing authorization
* Hidden admin endpoints
* Debug endpoints
* Development routes
* Export vulnerabilities
* Search vulnerabilities
* Repository bypasses
* Service bypasses

---

# Data Protection Review

Verify:

* Tenant isolation
* Repository isolation
* Service isolation
* Permission isolation
* API isolation
* Dashboard isolation

---

# Database Review

Verify customer data is protected by:

* PostgreSQL permissions
* Repository layer
* Service layer
* Authorization
* Multi-tenant enforcement

Document whether database encryption at rest is already provided by the hosting platform and identify any additional recommendations.
