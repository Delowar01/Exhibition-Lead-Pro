Enterprise AI Principles

Every AI capability must:

• Respect Enterprise Privacy
• Respect Multi-tenancy
• Respect RBAC
• Never leak customer data
• Never expose one tenant's information to another
• Never train shared models using customer business data
• Support configurable AI providers
• Support future model replacement

--------------------------------------------------

AI Architecture

Design an AI abstraction layer.

Do NOT tightly couple the application to a single AI provider.

Support future compatibility with providers such as:

• OpenAI
• Azure OpenAI
• Anthropic
• Google Gemini
• Local enterprise models

The initial implementation may use a single provider, but the architecture must remain provider-agnostic.

Execution Strategy

After creating the roadmap:

1. Present the complete Stage 5 Master Roadmap.
2. Explain the recommended implementation order.
3. Identify dependencies.
4. Identify phases that can run in parallel.
5. Recommend the appropriate Replit Agent mode (Power, Economy, Lite) for each phase.
6. Wait for approval before writing any production code.

Do not begin implementation until the roadmap has been reviewed and approved.
