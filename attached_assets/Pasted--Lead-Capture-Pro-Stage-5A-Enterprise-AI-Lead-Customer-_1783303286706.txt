# Lead Capture Pro
# Stage 5A – Enterprise AI Lead & Customer Intelligence

Objective

Stage 5.0 (AI Platform Foundation) has been successfully completed and approved.

Proceed with implementing Stage 5A – Enterprise AI Lead & Customer Intelligence.

This phase should transform CRM data into meaningful business intelligence while preserving the enterprise architecture already implemented.

Do NOT implement:

- AI Assistant
- Executive Intelligence
- Workflow Intelligence
- OCR Intelligence

Those belong to later Stage 5 phases.

This phase focuses only on Enterprise AI Intelligence for Leads, Contacts, Companies, Customer Relationships, and CRM Data Quality.

--------------------------------------------------

Requirements

Preserve:

• Existing business logic
• Enterprise Privacy
• Multi-tenancy
• RBAC
• Contract-first development
• Repository Layer
• Service Layer
• AI Platform Foundation

No regressions.

No breaking API contracts.

No breaking database changes unless required for AI metadata.

--------------------------------------------------

Enterprise AI Principles

Every AI recommendation must include:

• Confidence Score
• Generated Date
• Model Version
• Prompt Version
• Reasoning
• Last Analysis Date

AI must never silently overwrite CRM data.

AI recommendations must always be reviewable.

Users remain in control.

Accepted AI recommendations must be audited.

If confidence is insufficient, clearly display:

"Not enough information."

Never fabricate information.

10. AI Relationship Intelligence (NEW)

Implement Enterprise Relationship Intelligence.

Analyze relationships across the CRM.

Examples:

• Detect contacts belonging to the same company.
• Detect multiple decision makers within one organization.
• Identify repeat interactions across different exhibitions.
• Detect companies previously visited at other events.
• Identify existing customer relationships.
• Detect inactive customers requiring follow-up.
• Identify high-value companies interacting with multiple employees.
• Identify connected leads that should be managed together.

Display these insights in a dedicated Relationship Intelligence section.

Relationship suggestions must include:

• Confidence
• Reasoning
• Related Records
• Suggested Actions

Never automatically merge or modify records.
