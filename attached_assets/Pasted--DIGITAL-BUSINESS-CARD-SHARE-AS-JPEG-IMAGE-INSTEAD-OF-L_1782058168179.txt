# DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD – SHARE AS JPEG IMAGE INSTEAD OF LINK

## Project

AI Exhibition Lead Capture Platform

## Scope

**MOBILE APPLICATION ONLY**

(No Admin Panel changes)

Before implementing, review the existing Digital Business Card sharing workflow and ensure no regression occurs.

---

# Current Issue

The **Share** button on the Digital Business Card currently shares a **web link** to the public Digital Business Card.

This is **not** the desired behavior.

The Digital Business Card should be shared as a **high-quality JPEG image**, similar to sharing a poster, business card, or flyer.

---

# Required Behavior

When the user taps **Share**:

The application should:

1. Generate a high-resolution image of the Digital Business Card.
2. Save the image temporarily on the device.
3. Open the device's native Share Sheet.
4. Share the generated **JPEG image**, not a URL.

The shared content should be an actual image attachment.

Do **not** share:

* Public URL
* Landing page
* Web link
* HTML page
* QR page

The default behavior must always be sharing the JPEG image.

---

# Image Requirements

Generate the card exactly as displayed in the application.

Include:

* Profile Photo
* Name
* Designation
* Company Name
* Contact Numbers
* Email
* Address
* QR Code
* Application Logo inside the QR Code
* Company Branding

The image should look identical to the in-app Digital Business Card.

---

# Image Format

Generate:

* JPEG (.jpg)

High Quality

Recommended:

* 1080 × 1920 px (portrait)
* or higher resolution while maintaining the same aspect ratio

The image should remain sharp when shared through:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Telegram
* Messenger
* LinkedIn
* SMS
* AirDrop
* Quick Share
* Native Share

---

# QR Code

The QR Code inside the image must remain fully functional.

When someone scans the QR Code from the shared JPEG image:

* It should open the native Contact application.
* It should import the contact using the embedded vCard.
* It must **not** open a website.

---

# Sharing Workflow

User taps:

**Share**

↓

Generate JPEG

↓

Save to temporary storage

↓

Open Native Share Sheet

↓

User selects:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* SMS
* AirDrop
* Quick Share
* Messenger
* Any installed sharing application

↓

JPEG image is shared as an attachment.

---

# Temporary Files

After sharing:

* Remove temporary image files automatically.
* Prevent storage accumulation.

---

# Future Ready

Design the sharing service so it can later support:

* JPEG
* PNG
* PDF
* Wallet Pass
* Apple Wallet
* Google Wallet

without changing the Digital Business Card screen.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The task will be considered complete only when:

* The Share button no longer shares a web link.
* The Share button generates a high-quality JPEG image of the Digital Business Card.
* The JPEG matches the design shown inside the application.
* The QR Code remains fully scannable from the shared image.
* Scanning the QR Code opens the native Contact application and imports the contact using the embedded vCard.
* The native Share Sheet opens with the JPEG attached.
* Temporary files are cleaned up automatically after sharing.
* The feature works correctly on both Android and iPhone.
* Screenshots and testing evidence are provided before marking the task complete.
