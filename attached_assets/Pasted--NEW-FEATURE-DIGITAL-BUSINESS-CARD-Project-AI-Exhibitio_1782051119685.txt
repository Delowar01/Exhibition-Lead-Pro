# DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD MODULE



## Objective



Add a new module called **"My Digital Business Card"** inside the **Workspace** section.



This feature allows every user to create, edit, and share their own digital business card directly from the mobile application.



The generated digital business card will also become the main feature displayed on the Home screen, replacing the existing **"Capture a Lead"** section.



The UI must be premium, modern, elegant, responsive, and based on the attached HTML design. The attached design should be used as the visual reference while keeping it fully responsive for Android and iPhone.



---



# 1. Workspace Module



Add a new Workspace item:



**My Digital Business Card**



**Icon:**

Business Card / ID Card



### When opened



If no business card exists:



Display:



> **Create Your Digital Business Card**



If a business card already exists:



Open the View/Edit screen.



---



# 2. Business Card Information



Create a simple form containing only the following editable fields.



## Personal Information



* Full Name

* Designation

* Company Name

* Email Address

* Primary Contact Number

* Alternative Contact Number (Optional)

* Office Address



---



## Profile Photo



The Digital Business Card must **not** ask the user to upload a photo.



Instead:



* Automatically use the user's existing **Profile Avatar** from their account.

* Whenever the user updates their profile picture, the Digital Business Card should update automatically.

* There should be **no separate photo upload** inside the Digital Business Card module.



If no profile photo exists:



* Automatically generate a premium initials avatar.



---



## Optional Fields (Future Ready)



Prepare the database for future support of:



* Website

* LinkedIn

* Facebook

* Instagram

* X (Twitter)

* YouTube



These fields do not need to be visible in this iteration.



---



# 3. Premium QR Code



Automatically generate a QR Code for every Digital Business Card.



The QR Code should always open the user's public Digital Business Card.



Whenever the card information changes, the QR Code should automatically point to the latest version.



No manual QR upload should exist.



## QR Branding



The QR Code must follow premium branding standards.



Requirements:



* Place the **Application Logo** in the center of the QR Code.

* The logo should be surrounded by a white circular background for better visibility.

* Use a high error-correction level (Error Correction Level H) so the QR remains fully scannable even with the center logo.

* Maintain sufficient quiet zone (white border) around the QR Code.

* Logo size should be approximately **18–22%** of the QR Code size to preserve scan reliability.

* The QR Code must be tested on both Android and iPhone using multiple QR scanner applications.

* The QR Code should remain fully functional after embedding the logo.



The QR should have a premium branded appearance similar to modern digital business card applications.



---

Generate a premium public Digital Business Card based on the attached HTML design.

# 6. Edit Business Card



Users should be able to edit their Digital Business Card at any time.



Updating the card should immediately update:



* Home Screen

* Public Business Card

* QR Code

* Shared Link



No application restart should be required.



---



# 7. Share Feature



Provide a native Share button.



Support sharing via:



* WhatsApp

* Email

* SMS

* LinkedIn

* Native Device Share





The shared link should always open the latest version of the Digital Business Card.



---



# 8. Offline Support



Users must be able to:



* Create their Digital Business Card offline.

* Edit it offline.



All changes should synchronize automatically once internet connectivity is restored.



---



# 9. Security & Privacy



Only the fields configured for the Digital Business Card should be publicly visible.



Prepare the backend for future privacy controls, allowing users to choose which fields are publicly visible without requiring a database redesign.



---

Design the module so it can later support:

# Acceptance Criteria



The feature will be considered complete only when:



* A new **My Digital Business Card** module exists inside Workspace.

* Users can create, edit, and manage their Digital Business Card.

* The user's existing Profile Avatar is automatically used.

* No separate photo upload exists inside the Digital Business Card module.

* A premium initials avatar is generated automatically if no profile image exists.

* The Home Screen displays **My Digital Business Card** instead of **Capture a Lead**.

* A premium QR Code is generated automatically.

* The **Application Logo is embedded in the center of the QR Code** while remaining fully scannable.

* The public Digital Business Card follows the attached premium design.

* The sample **Save Contact** button is removed.

* Native Share works correctly on Android and iPhone.

* Offline create/edit works with automatic synchronization.

* Updates appear immediately throughout the application.

* The QR Code is successfully tested on Android and iPhone.

* Screenshots and a complete implementation summary are provided before marking the task as complete.
