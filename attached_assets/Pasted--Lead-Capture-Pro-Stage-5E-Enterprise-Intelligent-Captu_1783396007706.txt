# Stage 5E – Enterprise Intelligent Capture Engine

Stage 5.0, Stage 5A, Stage 5B, and Stage 5C have been successfully completed, validated, reviewed, and approved.

Proceed with Stage 5E – Enterprise Intelligent Capture Engine.

This stage must transform the existing Business Card Scanner into a world-class Enterprise Intelligent Capture platform.

The objective is not only to improve OCR accuracy, but to ensure every piece of information entering the CRM is validated, normalized, and intelligently reviewed before becoming part of the customer database.

• Existing Executive Intelligence

No regressions.

=========================================================
Enterprise AI Principles
=========================================================

Every OCR result must include:

• Confidence Score
• Processing Time
• AI Model
• Prompt Version
• Validation Status
• Extraction Method
• Generated Date

Never fabricate data.

If confidence is low, clearly indicate:

"Low Confidence"

or

"Not enough information"

instead of guessing.

Every suggestion must be reviewable.

Nothing should automatically overwrite CRM data.
