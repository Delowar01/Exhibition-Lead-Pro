# Lead Capture Pro – Stage 2.11A

# Enterprise Privacy Enforcement (Critical)

## Objective

Implement the Critical and High severity findings from the approved Enterprise Privacy & Data Governance Audit.

The goal is to remove the legacy "Super Admin" architecture and replace it with a true Enterprise SaaS Privacy Model where Platform Owner manages the platform but cannot access customer CRM data.

This phase focuses ONLY on security enforcement.

No UI redesign.

No database redesign.

No new features.

No regressions.

---

# Scope

Implement only the following audit findings:

* GAP-01
* GAP-02
* GAP-03

Do not work on Medium or Low findings yet.

---

# Requirements

## 1. Platform Owner Protection

Implement a dedicated authorization layer (for example, requireTenantUser() or a cleaner equivalent).

Platform Owner must receive HTTP 403 when attempting to access customer business data.

This protection must exist at the API layer.

UI hiding alone is NOT acceptable.

---

## 2. Protect Customer Business APIs

Protect every customer-data endpoint including:

* Contacts
* Leads
* Events
* Scans
* Business Card Images
* Reports
* CRM Analytics
* AI Enrichment
* Customer Attachments
* Customer Exports

Verify direct REST API calls are also blocked.

---

## 3. Reports Authorization

Review every reports endpoint.

Ensure:

* Platform Owner cannot access customer reports.
* Company Admin retains access.
* Company Employee follows RBAC.
* Existing customer functionality remains unchanged.

---

## 4. Business Card Images

Completely block Platform Owner access to business card image downloads.

---

## Testing

Add regression tests proving Platform Owner receives HTTP 403 for all protected endpoints.

Verify:

* Company Admin still works.
* Company Employee still works.
* Tenant isolation remains intact.
* Existing tests remain green.

---

## Deliverables

Provide:

* APIs protected
* Middleware added
* Tests added
* Security improvements
* Files modified
* Final validation summary

No regressions.
