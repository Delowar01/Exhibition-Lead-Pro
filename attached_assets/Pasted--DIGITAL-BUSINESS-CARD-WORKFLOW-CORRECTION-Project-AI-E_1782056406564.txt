# DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD WORKFLOW CORRECTION

## Project

AI Exhibition Lead Capture Platform

## Scope

**MOBILE APPLICATION ONLY**

(No Admin Panel changes)

---

# Current Issue

The current Digital Business Card workflow is incorrect.

The application has removed the ability to **Create** or **Edit** the Digital Business Card from the **Workspace**, and instead places editing functionality within the Dashboard flow.

This is not the intended user experience.

---

# Required Workflow

There must be a clear separation between **management** and **usage** of the Digital Business Card.

## Workspace

The **Workspace** is the only place where users can manage their Digital Business Card.

From **Workspace → My Digital Business Card**, users must be able to:

* Create a new Digital Business Card (if one does not exist)
* Edit an existing Digital Business Card
* Update profile information
* Configure public/private field visibility
* Enable or disable publishing
* Save changes

This is the **only location** where card management is allowed.

---

# Home Dashboard

The Home Dashboard is **not** a management screen.

Replace the current Digital Business Card preview with a simple action card:

### Title

**My Digital Business Card**

### Subtitle

**View and share your digital business card**

When tapped:

* Open the Digital Business Card page.

The Home Dashboard must **not** contain:

* Create button
* Edit button
* Form fields
* Visibility settings
* Publishing settings

---

# Digital Business Card Screen

The Digital Business Card screen opened from the Dashboard is **View & Share only**.

Users should be able to:

* View their Digital Business Card
* Share their Digital Business Card
* View the QR Code
* Share the QR Code

Users must **not** be able to:

* Create a card
* Edit a card
* Change visibility
* Change publishing status
* Modify any information

Remove:

* Edit icon
* Edit button
* Floating edit action
* Any shortcut that opens the editor

---

# First-Time User Experience

If the user has **never created** a Digital Business Card:

When they tap **My Digital Business Card** from the Home Dashboard:

Display a friendly empty-state message such as:

> **You haven't created your Digital Business Card yet.**

Include a single button:

**Go to Workspace**

This button should navigate directly to:

**Workspace → My Digital Business Card**

where the user can create their first card.

Do **not** allow card creation from the Dashboard flow.

---

# Navigation Flow

### Create/Edit

Workspace

→ My Digital Business Card

→ Create/Edit

→ Save

---

### View

Home Dashboard

→ My Digital Business Card

→ View & Share

---

# Single Source of Truth

There must be only **one** place in the application where the Digital Business Card can be created or edited:

**Workspace → My Digital Business Card**

This prevents duplicate workflows and keeps the application consistent.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The implementation will be considered complete only when:

* Users can **Create** a Digital Business Card only from **Workspace**.
* Users can **Edit** a Digital Business Card only from **Workspace**.
* The Home Dashboard contains only the **My Digital Business Card** action button.
* The Dashboard Digital Business Card screen supports **View** and **Share** only.
* No Edit icon or Edit button appears on the Dashboard Digital Business Card screen.
* If no Digital Business Card exists, the Dashboard directs the user to the Workspace instead of allowing creation there.
* All existing sharing functionality continues to work correctly.
* The workflow is consistent on Android and iPhone.
* Screenshots of the Workspace, Home Dashboard, and View-only Digital Business Card screen are provided before marking the task complete.
