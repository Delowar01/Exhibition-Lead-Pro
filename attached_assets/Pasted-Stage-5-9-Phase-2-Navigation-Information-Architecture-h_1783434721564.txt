Adaptive Enterprise UI
=========================================================

Do NOT build separate applications for different user roles.

• Pages
• Widgets
• Navigation
• Actions
• Reports
• Dashboards
• AI Features
• Quick Actions

• Sales Manager
• Employee

• Search

• Notifications
• AI Suggestions
• Reports
• Empty States

No permission logic should change.
