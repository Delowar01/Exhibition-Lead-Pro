# Lead Capture Pro
# Stage 5D – Enterprise AI Command Center (Final AI Phase)

Objective

Stage 5.0, Stage 5A, Stage 5B, Stage 5C, Stage 5E and Stage 5F have all been completed, validated and approved.

Proceed with Stage 5D.

This is the FINAL AI implementation phase.

The goal is NOT to build a simple chatbot.

The goal is to build the Enterprise AI Command Center for the entire platform.

The AI Command Center becomes the intelligent front-end for Lead Capture Pro.

It must orchestrate every AI capability already implemented while preserving Enterprise Privacy, Multi-tenancy, RBAC, and the Contract-first architecture.

=========================================================

Architecture Requirements

Preserve:

• Enterprise Privacy
• Multi-tenancy
• RBAC
• Contract-first Architecture
• Repository Layer
• Service Layer
• AI Platform Foundation
• AI Lead Intelligence
• AI Sales Copilot
• AI Executive Intelligence
• AI Intelligent Capture
• AI Workflow Intelligence

No regressions.

No breaking APIs.

No breaking business logic.

No breaking database schema unless absolutely required.

=========================================================

Enterprise AI Principles

The AI Command Center must NEVER:

• Invent information
• Guess missing facts
• Expose another tenant's information
• Bypass RBAC
• Automatically modify CRM data
• Automatically send Emails
• Automatically send WhatsApp messages
• Automatically assign leads
• Automatically close opportunities

Every action that changes data must require explicit user confirmation.

Every AI response must include:

• Confidence
• Model
• Prompt Version
• Timestamp
• Source
