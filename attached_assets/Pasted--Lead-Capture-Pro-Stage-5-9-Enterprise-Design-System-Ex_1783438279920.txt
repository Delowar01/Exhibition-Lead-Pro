# Lead Capture Pro
# Stage 5.9 – Enterprise Design System & Experience Modernization
# Phase 3B – Enterprise Workspace Modernization

Objective

Stage 5.9 Phase 3A has been completed, reviewed and approved.

Proceed with Phase 3B.

This phase focuses on redesigning the most frequently used workspaces in the application while preserving every existing feature.

This is NOT simply a UI redesign.

The goal is to create a premium Enterprise Workspace experience comparable to Salesforce, HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics.

=========================================================
Scope
=========================================================

Modernize ONLY:

• Lead Detail
• Company Detail
• Contact Detail
• Sales Pipeline
• AI Intelligence Workspace

Do NOT modify other modules.

=========================================================
Enterprise Workspace Principle
=========================================================

Lead Detail

Company Detail

Contact Detail

must belong to ONE Workspace Family.

Do NOT design three completely different pages.

Instead create one reusable Enterprise Workspace layout.

Users should instantly understand every page because they share the same design language and interaction model.

=========================================================
Workspace Layout
=========================================================

Every workspace should follow a common structure.

Top Area

• Entity Name
• Status
• Company
• Owner
• Important Badges
• Last Activity
• AI Indicator

=========================================================
Enterprise UX Rules
=========================================================

Every workspace must immediately answer:

1.

Where am I?

2.

What is most important?

3.

What should I do next?

Users should never search for important actions.

=========================================================

=========================================================

• Company Admin
