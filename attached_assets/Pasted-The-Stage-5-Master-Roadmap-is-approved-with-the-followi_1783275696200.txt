• Prompt Registry with version-controlled prompts.
• AI Settings (per tenant).
• AI Invocation Audit Ledger.
• AI usage tracking.
• AI token and cost tracking.
• Per-tenant AI configuration.
• Per-tenant feature toggles.
• Per-tenant usage limits.
• Per-tenant budget limits.
• Shared timeout handling.
• Shared retry strategy.
• Shared output validation.
• Shared error handling.
• AI provider health monitoring.
• AI logging.
• AI metrics.

Continue using Gemini as the only active provider.

The architecture must remain provider-agnostic so additional providers can be added later without changing business logic.

Do not implement multi-provider switching yet.
