# Lead Capture Pro
# Stage 5B – Enterprise AI Sales Copilot

Objective

Stage 5A – Enterprise AI Lead & Customer Intelligence has been completed and approved.

Proceed with Stage 5B – Enterprise AI Sales Copilot.

The goal is NOT simply to generate emails.

The goal is to create an Enterprise Sales Copilot that helps sales teams prepare, communicate, follow up, and close opportunities more effectively.

This phase must build upon the AI Platform Foundation (Stage 5.0) and AI Intelligence (Stage 5A).

Do NOT implement Executive Intelligence.

Do NOT implement AI Assistant.

Do NOT implement Workflow Intelligence.

Those belong to later phases.

--------------------------------------------------

Requirements

Preserve:

• Enterprise Privacy
• Multi-tenancy
• RBAC
• Contract-first architecture
• Repository Layer
• Service Layer
• AI Platform Foundation
• AI Lead Intelligence

No regressions.

No breaking API contracts.

No breaking business logic.

--------------------------------------------------

Enterprise AI Principles

Every AI response must include:

• Confidence Score
• Model
• Prompt Version
• Generated Date
• Reasoning (where applicable)

AI must never automatically send messages.

AI must never automatically update CRM data.

Users must always review and approve AI-generated content before sending or saving.

All AI actions must be recorded in the AI Audit Ledger.

Generate professional sales emails based on:

• Lead Intelligence
• Company Intelligence
• Contact Intelligence
• Relationship Intelligence
• Previous Activities
• Previous Notes
• Event Information

• English
• Arabic
